Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Personal WHOOP Health is a private application designed for personal health monitoring and analysis.

Information Collected

With the user's authorization, the application may access WHOOP health information, including heart rate, sleep, recovery, activity, and related metrics, depending on the permissions granted.

Use of Information

Health information is used to display personal health trends, analyze historical data, and provide personal insights. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and Security

Health information may be stored using Supabase with access controls. Reasonable security measures are used to protect personal information.

Third-Party Services

WHOOP provides the health data through authorized access. Supabase may provide backend storage and processing services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for personal historical analysis until it is deleted or access is revoked, subject to applicable technical and legal requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the application owner through the contact method provided during authorization.
